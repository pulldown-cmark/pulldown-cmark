Parser options: `ENABLE_SUPERSCRIPT | ENABLE_SUBSCRIPT`

# Superscript and subscript

Basic strikethrough is between two tildes:

```````````````````````````````` example
^This is super^ ~This is sub~
.
<p><sup>This is super</sup> <sub>This is sub</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
~This is stricken out~
.
<p><sub>This is stricken out</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

Backslash escapes:

```````````````````````````````` example
~This is \~stricken~
.
<p><sub>This is ~stricken</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
~This~is~nothing~
.
<p><sub>This</sub>is<sub>nothing</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example ENABLE_STRIKETHROUGH
~This ~~is not stricken.~
.
<p><sub>This ~~is not stricken.</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
~This ~~is not stricken.~
.
<p><sub>This ~~is not stricken.</sub></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example ENABLE_STRIKETHROUGH
~~This ~is~~ stricken.~
.
<p><del>This ~is</del> stricken.~</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
~~This ~is~~ stricken.~
.
<p>~~This ~is~~ stricken.~</p>
````````````````````````````````

The first one wins.

```````````````````````````````` example ENABLE_STRIKETHROUGH
~This ~~is stricken~ but this is not~~
.
<p><sub>This ~~is stricken</sub> but this is not~~</p>
````````````````````````````````

Though strikethrough requires left and right flanking, subscript does not.
Neither does superscript.

```````````````````````````````` example
H~2~O

y=x^2^a+xb+c
.
<p>H<sub>2</sub>O</p>
<p>y=x<sup>2</sup>a+xb+c</p>
````````````````````````````````

Superscript and subscript cannot group with a different delimiter count.

```````````````````````````````` example
~foo~~

^bar^^
.
<p>~foo~~</p>
<p>^bar^^</p>
````````````````````````````````

The lower bound of superscript and subscript are separate.
Emphasis example included for analogy.

```````````````````````````````` example
~foo^~^bar~

*foo_*_bar*
.
<p><sub>foo^</sub>^bar~</p>
<p><em>foo_</em>_bar*</p>
````````````````````````````````

Superscript with punctuation content (chemical ions, math).
When preceded by an alphanumeric character, superscript opens
even if the content is punctuation like `+` or `-`.

```````````````````````````````` example
H^+^ + OH^-^
.
<p>H<sup>+</sup> + OH<sup>-</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
Ca^2+^ + CO~3~^2-^
.
<p>Ca<sup>2+</sup> + CO<sub>3</sub><sup>2-</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
NH~4~^+^
.
<p>NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup></p>
````````````````````````````````

Standalone punctuation-only superscript without a preceding
alphanumeric does not open (avoids false positives).

```````````````````````````````` example
^+^ not superscript
.
<p>^+^ not superscript</p>
````````````````````````````````
